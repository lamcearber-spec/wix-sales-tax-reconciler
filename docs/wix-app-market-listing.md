## Short Description

Reconcile US sales tax by state and jurisdiction before filing.

sales tax by state, tax reconciliation, state filing report, sales tax report, Wix tax report, accounting, tax evidence, tax variance

## Full Description

Sales Tax Reconciler gives Wix merchants a read-only, filing-period report for US sales tax collected versus expected by ship-to jurisdiction.

The app reviews orders, line-item tax details, and payment/refund transaction data, then produces advisory jurisdiction summaries, variance checks, flagged orders, CSV exports, and a return-ready PDF for accountant review.

Every figure is advisory and should be confirmed before filing. The app never writes to your Wix store and never files tax returns.
